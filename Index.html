1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including name, email address, and payment details when you register or subscribe. We also collect usage data such as quiz attempts, scores, and activity logs to improve your experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the QuizMaster Pro platform
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions
  • To send transactional emails (OTP, receipts, alerts)
  • To improve AI quiz generation based on usage patterns
  • To enforce our anti-cheat and proctoring policies

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase PostgreSQL with row-level security (RLS). Passwords are hashed using bcryptjs with 12 salt rounds. We use JWT authentication with 7-day expiry for all sessions.

4. Payment Data

Payments are processed via Razorpay. We do not store your card details. All transactions are encrypted and handled by Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.

5. Cookies

We use session cookies for authentication and localStorage for user preferences. No third-party advertising cookies are used.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with service providers (Razorpay, Groq AI, Supabase) solely to operate the platform.
